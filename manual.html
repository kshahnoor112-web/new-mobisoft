1. POS Terminal & Fast Barcode / IMEI Billing (Counter Sale Charhana)

Maqsad (Purpose): Counter par customer ko bina kisi rukawat ke 5 second ke andar mobile phone ya accessory bechna, exact IMEI lock karna aur bill banana.

Is Function Ka Kaam Aur Zaroori Features:

  • Instant Barcode / IMEI Scanner Interceptor: Cashier USB ya Bluetooth scanner se mobile ke dabbe par laga 15-digit IMEI scan kare ya accessory ka barcode scan kare, bina mouse chuye system carriage return pakar kar foran item cart mein daal deta hai.
  • Specific IMEI Selector (Serial Lock): Agar ek hi model (maslan Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra) ke dukan par 3 dabbe mojood hain, to cart mein drop-down se wahi IMEI select ho jata hai jo customer ko physical dabbe par diya gaya hai.
  • Customer Contact Registration: Customer ka Name aur WhatsApp/SMS Phone number darj kiya jata hai taakeh future warranty claim par pehchan ho sake.
  • Dual Discount System: Har item par line-level discount (maslan ₹500 off) ya pooray bill par total bill discount diya ja sakta hai.
  • Multi-Tender Payments: Cash, Card / POS Terminal, aur Online Transfer (EasyPaisa, JazzCash, Raast, Bank).
  • Change Due Calculator: Customer se aane walay rupaye (Cash Tendered) likhein, system foran bache hue paise hisab karke bata deta hai taakeh cashier se koi hisab ki ghalti na ho.

2. 80mm & 58mm Thermal Receipt Engine (Parchi Printing)

Maqsad (Purpose): Sale complete hotay hi standard POS thermal printer se customer ko pakki parchi nikaal kar dena jo warranty ke waqt kaam aati hai.

Parchi Par Kya Kya Print Hota Hai:
  • Dukan Ki Branding: Shop Name, Branch Address, Phone Number aur NTN/STRN tax number.
  • Unique Invoice ID: Auto-generated serial number (maslan INV-2026-0002).
  • Exact IMEI Number: Beche gaye mobile ka 15-digit IMEI number parchi par saaf print hota hai. Kal ko customer warranty ya repair ke liye aaye to dukan ka IMEI match kiya ja sake.
  • Bill Breakdown: Gross Subtotal, Discount, Tax, Net Payable, Cash Received aur Change Due.
  • Official Warranty Policy: 1-Year brand warranty ke qawaneen aur sharaait.
  • Barcode: Parchi ke aakhir par invoice ka barcode aata hai taakeh return ke waqt scanner se parchi scan ho sake.
  • WhatsApp Share: Parchi ka text 1-click se clipboard mein copy kar ke customer ke WhatsApp par bheja ja sakta hai.

3. Inventory & 15-Digit IMEI Lifecycle Tracking (Stock Charhana & Serial Audit)

Maqsad (Purpose): Dukan ke har ek dabbe aur piece ka hisab rakhna taakeh stock chori (shrinkage) ya ghalat number bikne ka khatra khatam ho jaye.

Is Function Ka Kaam:

  • Add Stock Modal: Naye dabbe aane par Model Name, Brand, Cost Price (Khareed), Selling Price, aur Barcode darj karein.
  • Bulk IMEI Entry: "Track Unique Serial / IMEI Numbers" check karein aur jitne dabbe aaye hain unke 15-digit IMEIs scan ya paste kar dein. Har IMEI system mein alag register ho jata hai.
  • IMEI Status Lifecycle:
    • Available: Stock mein mojood hai, counter par becha ja sakta hai.
    • Sold: Bik chuka hai (kis date ko bika aur kis invoice number par bika, sab record rehta hai).
    • Returned: Customer ne kisi waja se wapis kiya.
  • Double-Selling Prevention: Agar koi IMEI pehle se Sold hai to system usko dobara kisi aur bill mein add hone nahi deta.
  • Low Stock Warnings: Agar kisi phone ya charger ka stock minimum safe threshold (maslan 2 pieces) par pohnch jaye to system yellow warning badge dikhata hai.

4. Daily Closing, Net Profit & Financial Analytics (Sham Ka Hisab & Saaf Munafa)

Maqsad (Purpose): Sham ko dukan band karne se pehle maalik ko ek second mein pata chal jaye ke aaj din bhar ka asal saaf munafa kitna hua.

Financial Metric Matlab / Formula Maalik Ko Faida
Gross Sales Revenue Aaj din bhar mein kul kitne rupaye ke mobile aur samaan bika. Cash inflow ka pata chalta hai.
Cost of Goods (COGS) Aaj beche gaye items ki wholesale khareed qeemat kitni thi. Asal capital cost nikal aati hai.
Net Realized Profit Gross Sales - Cost Price = Asal Saaf Munafa Dukan ka asal rozana ka profit wazeh hota hai.
Profit Margin % (Net Profit / Gross Sales) * 100 Karobaar ki sehat aur margin ka andaza hota hai.
Cash Drawer vs Digital Cash Counter + Card Machine + EasyPaisa/JazzCash Tijori / Galle mein cash count karne mein madad milti hai.

5. Sales History & 1-Click Lost Slip Reprinting (Pichla Record)

Maqsad (Purpose): Pichli tamam sales ka ledger dekhna aur agar customer slip ghum kar de to dobara print nikaal kar dena.

  • Instant Search: Invoice Number (jaise INV-2026-0001), customer ke phone number, ya mobile ke IMEI number se search karein.
  • 1-Click Reprint: Kisi bhi invoice ke aage "Reprint Slip" ka button dabayein, waisi hi thermal parchi khul jayegi.
  • Cashier Audit Trail: Kis cashier ne sale banayi aur kis waqt banayi, sab date/time ke sath mehfooz rehta hai.

6. Multi-Branch Network & 7. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Multi-Branch Network

Agar aapki ek se zyada shops hain (maslan Main Flagship Blue Area, Mall Road Lahore, aur Saddar Karachi), to top bar se foran branch badal kar us branch ka alag stock, sales, aur cash counter hisab dekh sakte hain.

Role-Based Access (RBAC)

Cashier: Counter par sirf sale charha sakta hai. Wholesale khareed qeemat aur dukan ka munafa cashier se chupa rehta hai.
Store Manager & Admin: Naya stock daal sakte hain, khareed qeemat dekh sakte hain aur sham ka munafa report dekh sakte hain.
